Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026 • Ammi Enterprises, Mumbai – 400028

Ammi Enterprises (“we”, “us”, “Ammikids”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Policy explains what data we collect when you use the Ammikids retail storefront and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

  • Order information: name, email, phone, shipping address, and payment status provided at checkout (via Saleor checkout API ammikids.themanagemate.com/graphql/).
  • Device & usage: browser type, IP address, pages viewed, search queries — used for analytics and fraud prevention.
  • On-device storage: cart (ak_cart) and wishlist (ak_wish) are stored in your browser’s localStorage; they are not synced to our servers unless you place an order.

2. How we use your information

  • To process, pack, ship and support your orders (including COD verification via phone/WhatsApp).
  • To communicate order updates, returns/refunds, and respond to enquiries at ammi.kidz@gmail.com.
  • To improve our catalogue, sizing (size charts) and storefront experience.

3. Sharing

We share data only with service providers required to run the store: Saleor (commerce backend), payment gateways (for prepaid/UPI), and courier partners for delivery. We do not sell your personal data.

4. Cookies & similar

We use essential cookies/localStorage for cart, wishlist, and session. Analytics (if enabled) uses anonymised data. You may clear localStorage/cookies from your browser at any time.

5. Data retention

Order records are retained as required for GST, accounting and warranty/returns (typically 8 years). Marketing data is kept until you unsubscribe.

6. Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by emailing ammi.kidz@gmail.com with your order email/phone. We will respond within 7 working days per applicable Indian law.

7. Children’s privacy

Products are for kids, but purchases must be made by an adult (18+). We do not knowingly collect data directly from children.
