Refund Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026 • All refunds in INR to the original payment method

We want you to love every Ammikids outfit. If something isn’t right, here’s how refunds work. This policy is to be read with our Return Policy and Shipping Policy.

1. Eligibility

  • Refund is issued only after a return is received, inspected and approved (see Return Policy — 7 days from delivery, unused/unwashed with tags).
  • Orders cancelled before dispatch are eligible for a full refund. COD orders have no payment to refund.
  • Sale / discounted / “final sale” items may be exchange-only where marked at checkout.

2. Timelines

  • Once approved, refunds are initiated within 2–3 working days.
  • Prepaid / UPI / card: credited in 5–7 working days by your bank. Wallet refunds (if any) are instant.
  • COD refunds (where an advance was paid) are sent via UPI/bank transfer after verification.
Note: Refund timelines are counted from approval, not from the day you ship the return. Courier transit is extra. For Saleor checkout checkoutCreate demo token orders, no real charge is made — no refund needed.

3. How refunds are paid

  • To the original payment method — same UPI ID / card / account used at checkout.
  • Shipping charges are non-refundable, except where the return is due to our error (wrong/defective item). Any COD fee collected is non-refundable if you refuse delivery without cause.

4. Non-refundable cases

  • Items returned without tags, washed/worn, with perfume/alteration, or after 7 days.
  • Innerwear / socks / accessories where hygiene applies (as marked on the PDP).
  • Damage caused by improper washing or handling after delivery.

5. Damaged / wrong item

If you receive a defective or wrong-size item, email ammi.kidz@gmail.com within 48 hours with order ID + photos/video of the parcel and product. We will arrange a free replacement or full refund including shipping.
