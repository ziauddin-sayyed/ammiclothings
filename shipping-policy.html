Shipping Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026 • Pan-India shipping from Mumbai 400028

We ship Ammikids kidswear across India from Mumbai. Orders are packed at A-7, Kasturchand Mill Compound and handed to our courier partners. Tracking is shared via SMS/email/WhatsApp once dispatched.

1. Coverage & courier

We deliver to all serviceable pincodes via reputed couriers (Delhivery / Shiprocket / India Post where courier is unserviceable). Remote-area deliveries may take longer or be routed via Post.

2. Timelines

StageTime
Order processing & packing1–2 working days (Mon–Sat)
Transit — Metro cities2–4 working days after dispatch
Transit — Rest of India3–6 working days after dispatch
Remote / NE / J&K5–8 working days after dispatch
Timelines are estimates excluding Sundays and public holidays. Delays due to weather, courier load, festivals, or address issues are beyond our control but we will keep you updated on your tracking ID.

3. Shipping charges

Shipping fees (where applicable) are displayed at checkout before payment. For returns/exchanges, see Return Policy.

4. Cash on Delivery (COD)

  • COD is available on most pincodes up to a value limit set by the courier. For high-value or remote-pincode orders COD may be disabled — please choose Prepaid/UPI.
  • COD orders are verified by phone/WhatsApp. Please keep your phone reachable — unverified COD orders may be cancelled.

5. Tracking & address

  • Tracking link is shared once shipped. Please ensure the address, pincode and phone number are correct — any re-dispatch due to wrong address will attract fresh shipping charges.
  • If the courier reports “customer not reachable / door locked ”, we will attempt redelivery per courier policy before marking RTO (return to origin).
