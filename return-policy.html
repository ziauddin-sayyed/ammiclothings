Return & Exchange Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026 • 7-day returns • Mumbai 400028

We stitch for twirls — and fit matters. You may return or exchange eligible kidswear within 7 days of delivery.

1. Eligibility

  • Request must be raised within 7 days of delivery (courier “delivered” timestamp).
  • Item must be unused, unwashed, unworn with original tags, packaging and freebies intact.
  • Sale / final-sale items are exchange-only where marked. Innerwear/socks/accessories are non-returnable for hygiene.

2. Non-returnable

  • Washed, worn, perfumed, altered, or damaged after delivery.
  • Items without tags, or with missing set pieces (co-ord sets must be returned as a complete set).
  • Orders marked as “non-returnable” on the PDP at purchase.

3. How to request

Step 1 — Write to us

Email ammi.kidz@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91-9321508566 with order ID, reason and 2–3 photos/video.

Step 2 — Approval

We reply within 24–48 hours with an RMA, pickup address and return instructions. Do not self-ship without approval.

Step 3 — Pickup & inspect

Courier picks up (where serviceable). We inspect within 2 working days of receipt and then exchange/refund per Refund Policy.

4. Exchange

Size/colour exchange is subject to stock. If the requested variant is unavailable you may choose another style or opt for a refund (where eligible). One exchange per order; price difference (if any) is adjusted at dispatch.

5. Return shipping

  • For our error (wrong/defective/damaged): we bear both-way shipping.
  • For size/fit/preference change: return shipping is borne by the customer. Original shipping/COD fees are non-refundable.
